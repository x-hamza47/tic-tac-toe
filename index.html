Play Tic Tac Toe online against the computer

This free Tic Tac Toe game runs in your browser with no download or signup. Choose to play as X or O and pick a difficulty: Easy makes random moves, Medium mixes smart and random moves, and Hard never loses.

How to play

Take turns placing your mark on a 3 by 3 grid. The first player to get three marks in a row, column or diagonal wins. If all nine squares are filled with no winner, the game is a draw.
