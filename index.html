How to Play Tic Tac Toe Online

This free Tic Tac Toe game runs in your browser, with no download or signup. Play against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, as X or O, on your phone or desktop.

The rules

  1. Pick a difficulty, then choose to play as X or O.
  2. Players take turns placing a mark in an empty square of the 3 by 3 grid. X always goes first.
  3. Get three of your marks in a row, column or diagonal to win.
  4. If all nine squares are filled and nobody has three in a row, the game is a draw.

Difficulty levels

  • Easy: the computer plays random moves. Good for beginners and kids.
  • Medium: the computer usually wins or blocks when it can, but sometimes makes mistakes. You can beat it with a fork, which is two winning threats at once.
  • Hard: the computer never loses. The best result you can get is a draw.

Tic Tac Toe strategy tips

  • Start in a corner or the center, since these squares belong to the most winning lines.
  • Always check whether your opponent has two in a row, and block the third square.
  • Try to create a fork: a move that gives you two ways to win at once.
  • Against a perfect opponent, correct play from both sides always ends in a draw.

Frequently asked questions

Can I beat the computer at Tic Tac Toe?

Yes on Easy and Medium. On Hard the computer never loses, so a draw is the best you can do.

Who goes first?

X always goes first. If you choose O, the computer plays X and makes the opening move.

Is this game free?

Yes. It is free, needs no signup, and runs directly in your browser.

What is the best first move?

A corner or the center. Both give you the most chances to win.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The game is responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.
